# ⚖️ Legal Assistance & Document Analysis App

A comprehensive **Legal Assistance Platform** designed to make legal information and services more accessible. The application brings together **laws, policies, constitutional articles, document analysis, OCR, and lawyer consultation** in a single platform.

## 🚀 Overview

This application acts as a digital legal assistant that helps users understand legal information, analyze legal documents, and connect with lawyers based on their location.

Users can search and explore **laws, government policies, legal provisions, and constitutional articles**, while also using OCR technology to extract and analyze text from uploaded documents and PDFs.

The platform also provides a convenient way to **find lawyers in the user's area, view their profiles, and book appointments** for legal consultation.

## ✨ Key Features

### 📚 Laws, Policies & Articles

* Browse and search legal information.
* Access laws, policies, rules, and regulations.
* Explore constitutional articles and legal provisions.
* Search for relevant legal topics from a centralized platform.

### 📄 OCR Document Analysis

* Upload images or PDF documents.
* Extract text from scanned or image-based documents using **OCR (Optical Character Recognition)**.
* Convert physical/legal documents into searchable digital text.
* Analyze extracted content to help users understand important information within their documents.

### 🤖 Legal Assistance

* Helps users identify relevant laws and legal provisions.
* Provides explanations of legal information in a user-friendly format.
* Helps users understand important sections and terminology present in legal documents.

> **Disclaimer:** The application is designed for informational and educational purposes and should not be considered a replacement for professional legal advice.

### 👨‍⚖️ Find Lawyers

* Discover lawyers based on the user's location.
* View lawyer information and profiles.
* Find lawyers according to relevant legal requirements.
* Make it easier for users to connect with legal professionals.

### 📅 Appointment Booking

* View available lawyer consultation slots.
* Book appointments directly through the application.
* Manage scheduled consultations.
* Simplifies the process of connecting users with legal professionals.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to **simplify access to legal information and legal services** by combining legal resources, OCR-based document processing, and lawyer consultation into one application.

Instead of requiring users to search through multiple websites, manually read complex documents, or separately find a lawyer, the platform provides these functionalities through a single system.
